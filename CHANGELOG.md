## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-19

### Added

#### Foundational research papers

- [`SSI-001 — From Structural Intelligence to Subjective Structural Intelligence`](docs/SSI-001-From-Structural-Intelligence-to-Subjective-Structural-Intelligence.md): introduces SSI's scope and the question **“Whose choice is it?”**
- [`SSI-002 — Subject, Boundary, Embodiment, and Historical Identity`](docs/SSI-002-Subject-Boundary-Embodiment-and-Historical-Identity.md): develops operational subject boundaries and historically grounded identity.
- [`SSI-003 — Perception, Localization, and the Subjective World Model`](docs/SSI-003-Perception-Localization-and-the-Subjective-World-Model.md): examines situated perception and subject-relative interpretation.
- [`SSI-004 — Scoring Forests, Valuation, and Worldview Formation`](docs/SSI-004-Scoring-Forests-Valuation-and-Worldview-Formation.md): distinguishes computational scoring from the provenance and authority of evaluation criteria.
- [`SSI-005 — Structural Agency, Choice, and Functional Freedom`](docs/SSI-005-Structural-Agency-Choice-and-Functional-Freedom.md): proposes an operational account of alternatives, constraints, and accountable choice.
- [`SSI-006 — Self-Referential Scoring, Self-Models, and Metacognition`](docs/SSI-006-Self-Referential-Scoring-Self-Models-and-Metacognition.md): explores reflective evaluation, self-model evidence, and revision controls.
- [`SSI-007 — Recursive Principle Formation and Worldview Evolution`](docs/SSI-007-Recursive-Principle-Formation-and-Worldview-Evolution.md): studies the governed evolution of principles and worldview structures.
- [`SSI-008 — Consciousness Boundaries and the Future of SSI`](docs/SSI-008-Consciousness-Boundaries-and-the-Future-of-SSI.md): defines limits on claims about consciousness, experience, and moral status.

#### Research cases

- [`CASE-001 — One Environment, Multiple Subjects`](cases/CASE-001-One-Environment-Multiple-Subjects.md): contrasting subject-relative interpretations of a common environment.
- [`CASE-002 — Human–AI Delegation and Scoring Sovereignty`](cases/CASE-002-Human-AI-Delegation-and-Scoring-Sovereignty.md): evaluation authority and traceable delegation in human–AI systems.
- [`CASE-003 — Worldview Evolution and Identity Preservation`](cases/CASE-003-Worldview-Evolution-and-Identity-Preservation.md): governed principle revision under changing evidence while preserving historical accountability.

#### Conceptual structures and distinctions

- Introduced or consolidated candidate structures including **HIG**, **SWM**, **SRVC**, **CPG**, **SMEL**, **REG**, **MCC**, **RRG**, **PEG**, **WRM**, and **RPGC**.
- Distinguished **subject from owner**, **scoring from feeling**, **capability from authority**, **agency from metaphysical free will**, and **self-model from consciousness**.
- Positioned SSI as complementary to, but conceptually distinct from, the **Universal Control Plane and Scoring Forest (UCPSF)** research direction.
- Adopted an evidence-oriented research posture: constructs are hypotheses and design proposals unless independently validated.

### Research status

Version 1.0.0 is an **initial conceptual research release candidate**. Its mechanisms, experiments, and case studies should not be interpreted as validated empirical findings, implemented software, or evidence of phenomenal consciousness in AI systems.

The date above records the preparation of this initial research snapshot. It **does not assert** that a GitHub release, Zenodo deposit, DOI registration, or ResearchGate publication has already occurred. Those publication events should be recorded when verified.

## Release maintenance policy

- **Added**: new papers, cases, figures, definitions, or research assets.
- **Changed**: substantive revisions to constructs, assumptions, experiments, or interpretations.
- **Deprecated**: terminology or structures retained for historical traceability but no longer recommended.
- **Removed**: withdrawn material, with a rationale and archival reference where feasible.
- **Fixed**: broken links, figure references, metadata, formatting, and factual errors.
- **Security / Governance**: changes affecting authorization, evaluation sovereignty, or revision safeguards, where applicable.

A revision that materially changes the meaning of an SSI construct should identify the previous definition, new definition, rationale, and affected documents. Contradictory evidence should be recorded rather than silently incorporated into a revised narrative.
