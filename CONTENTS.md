## 1. Repository at a Glance

**Central question: _Whose choice is it?_**

Subjective Structural Intelligence (SSI) investigates how identifiable subjects, their boundaries, histories, capabilities, values, authorization relationships, and evolving worldviews condition perception, evaluation, choice, reflection, and structural growth. It complements—but is distinct from—the Universal Control Plane and Scoring Forest (UCPSF) research program. UCPSF concerns scoring and control architectures; SSI asks whose perspectives and values they represent, and who has legitimate authority over their use and revision.

This repository contains **eight core research papers**, **three research cases**, and **seven planned figure files**. The proposed constructs are research candidates, not validated universal standards. Functional subjecthood and self-modeling are not evidence, by themselves, of phenomenal consciousness.

For a short introduction, read [README.md](README.md). For a guided reading sequence, see [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md).

## 5. Cross-Cutting Structural Constructs

The research program uses several candidate constructs across papers and cases:

| Construct | Purpose | Main references |
|---|---|---|
| **HIG — Historical Identity Graph** | Trace identity-relevant history, provenance, and structural transformations. | SSI-002, SSI-007, CASE-003 |
| **SWM — Subjective World Model** | Represent the environment as interpreted by a situated subject. | SSI-003, CASE-001 |
| **SRVC — Subject-Relative Valuation Contract** | Associate evaluative criteria with subjects, contexts, provenance, and authority. | SSI-004, CASE-002 |
| **CPG — Choice Provenance Graph** | Trace why a choice occurred and how authorization and consequences relate. | SSI-005, CASE-002 |
| **SMEL — Self-Model Evidence Ledger** | Ground self-assessment in evidence and uncertainty. | SSI-006 |
| **REG — Reflective Evaluation Graph** | Track evaluator dependencies, challenges, and possible circularity. | SSI-006 |
| **MCC — Metacognitive Control Contract** | Govern when and how reflective evaluation occurs. | SSI-006 |
| **RRG — Reflective Revision Gate** | Separate proposed changes, validation, authorization, and deployment. | SSI-006, SSI-007 |
| **PEG — Principle Evolution Graph** | Track formation, challenge, revision, and replacement of principles. | SSI-007, CASE-003 |
| **WRM — Worldview Revision Map** | Trace changes in worldview structure and evaluative priorities. | SSI-007, CASE-003 |
| **CCBP — Consciousness Claim Boundary Protocol** | Separate functional evidence from unsupported consciousness claims. | SSI-008 |

These names denote proposed analytical or engineering interfaces. They should not be presented as independently validated metrics or mandatory components of every intelligent system.

## 7. Research Boundaries

The SSI research program maintains the following distinctions:

- **Subject ≠ Owner:** ownership does not establish a subject's functional identity.
- **Perception ≠ Raw Input:** interpretation is situated and context-dependent.
- **Perspective ≠ Truth:** subject-relative interpretations remain answerable to evidence.
- **Scoring ≠ Feeling:** evaluation mechanisms do not establish phenomenal experience.
- **Capability ≠ Authority:** being able to calculate, decide, or execute does not establish permission.
- **Agency ≠ Metaphysical Free Will:** functional agency is an operational research concept.
- **Self-Model ≠ Consciousness:** self-representation alone cannot establish sentience.
- **Historical Continuity ≠ Numerical Personal Identity:** traceable causal history does not settle philosophical identity.
- **Worldview Change ≠ Improvement:** revisions require independent evidence, counter-evidence, and governance.

**Evidence status:** SSI offers candidate architectures, conceptual distinctions, and testable hypotheses. No universal subject detector, consciousness test, or empirically validated SSI benchmark is claimed.

## 8. Relationship to Structural Intelligence and UCPSF

SSI builds on Structural Intelligence concepts including MDT, UTN, CBT, CCC, Calling Graphs, Scoring Forests, Principle Intelligence, structural learning, and governed recursive improvement.

The conceptual division is:

```text
UCPSF:  How are alternatives scored, checked, and controlled?
SSI:    For whom are alternatives meaningful, whose values govern,
        who may choose, and how does the subject evolve?
```

The two programs are complementary research directions and remain separate DOI repositories. Their interaction is an architectural proposal rather than evidence of a completed integrated runtime.
